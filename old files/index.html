How it works

  1. Tell us what you want

    Message us on WhatsApp with a photo of the style, your fabric (or ask us to source it) and your event date.

  2. Share your measurements

    Visit us, or send measurements. We’ll guide you through taking them at home.

  3. Agree the price and date

    We confirm the cost and pickup date, and take a deposit to start.

  4. Collect your outfit

    Try it on, and we adjust the fit before you leave.
